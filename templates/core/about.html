About Hometrace

A modern real estate platform simplifying property discovery, student accommodation, and transactions in Keffi, Nasarawa State.

Our Mission

Hometrace eliminates the opacity and fraud in property search and student housing across Keffi and Nasarawa State. We provide a transparent marketplace where students, families, civil servants, and property buyers connect directly with legitimate agents, explore verified listings, and transact with peace of mind.
