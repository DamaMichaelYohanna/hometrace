Hometrace Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Hometrace ("we", "our", or "the Platform") respects your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding user information in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and international security standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly upon registering as a Property Agent or Buyer/Renter, including your full name, email address, telephone contact number, agency details, and uploaded property media.

2. Payment & Financial Data

All digital payments conducted through Hometrace (including agent verification and inspection deposit fees) are processed directly by our PCI-DSS compliant partner Paystack. Hometrace does not store debit card details, CVVs, or bank account PINs on our servers.

3. Use of Progressive Web App (PWA) Storage

Our service worker caches application stylesheets, icons, and shell layouts on your device to ensure reliable performance during intermittent network conditions in Nigeria.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to request account deletion, please contact privacy@hometrace.ng.
